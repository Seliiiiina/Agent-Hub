## Description

Explains quality-system fundamentals from approved applicable sources with citations, scope limits, and unresolved questions. Use when clarifying quality concepts, processes, or terminology. Returns a concise knowledge explanation, citations, applicability, unknowns, and any boundary reason. Do not add an action plan or professional determination.
